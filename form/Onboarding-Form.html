📋 How to find or get your EIN
  1. Already have one? Check the IRS confirmation letter you received (Form CP 575), or look on any business tax return you've filed.
  2. Don't have one yet? Apply free and instantly at irs.gov → Apply for an EIN Online. Takes about 5 minutes. You get your number immediately.
  3. If you're a sole proprietor with no employees, you technically can use your Social Security Number instead — but getting an EIN is free and keeps your SSN private.

🌐 How to choose a good domain name
  1. Keep it short — ideally under 20 characters. Less typing = easier for customers.
  2. Use your business name + city or service type if it's too common. E.g., burkeshvacstl.com
  3. Stick to .com if possible — it's what people type automatically.
  4. Check if it's available: namecheap.com — type the name in the search bar.
  5. Don't buy it yourself — just pick your top 2 choices and we'll handle the purchase.

📍 What is a Google Business Profile and how do I get one?
  1. A Google Business Profile (GBP) is the listing that shows up on Google Maps and in search results when someone looks for your business or services nearby. It's free.
  2. Go to business.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  3. Click "Add your business" → enter your business name and category (HVAC Contractor) → choose "Service area business" since you don't have a storefront.
  4. Enter your service areas → add your phone number and website (leave website blank for now).
  5. Choose a verification method — Google may ask for a video walkthrough or a postcard to your address. The postcard takes 5–14 days. Start this as early as possible.
  6. Once verified, we'll collect your Google review link and wire it into your system.
